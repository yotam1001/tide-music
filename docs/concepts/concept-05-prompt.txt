Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity landscape desktop app concept
Primary request: Create a wide charcoal Windows music library called Tide. The screen must visibly be a desktop library, not a player or queue. Main layout is three vertical panes: narrow left navigation; broad center with album grid and song table; narrow right selected-album details. Bottom full-width transport bar. Album-first hierarchy.
Composition/framing: Wide landscape 16:10 app window, spanning most of the image. Left rail about 17% width with “Library”, “Albums”, “Playlists”, “Recently Added”. Center about 62% width with “Albums” header, one row of four compact album cover cards with names/artists, then “All Songs” table with five tracks and columns for title, artist, album and duration. Right pane about 21% width labeled “Playing”, a small cover, track title, artist, and short album details. A clear bottom transport bar across app with play/pause, previous/next, elapsed time, progress and volume. Beneath main window, show a small separate horizontal mini-player strip.
Style: polished native Windows desktop UI, restrained editorial typography, flat matte charcoal and soft near-black surfaces, readable off-white labels, thin separators. Accent ONLY deep burgundy for active nav, selected row and progress. No blue/cyan/teal.
Album covers: small abstract geometric album covers in a tasteful editorial style.
Avoid: Any portrait layout, narrow centered player, queue screen, giant player controls, large featured cover, cyan, teal, blue accent, YouTube brand, neon, glass, waveform, device metrics, claims, watermark.
