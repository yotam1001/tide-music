Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished FULL 1200x800 desktop native music application screen for Grove, a lightweight Spotify player. This is a design specification for implementation in Rust/Slint native controls, not a website or marketing landing page. User rejected a muddy pine-green UI; make the whole design look much better through neutral ink/charcoal surfaces and precisely used vivid emerald green. Exact locked colors: main canvas #141617 neutral dark charcoal, left rail #0b0d0e almost black, bottom player #0b0d0e, fields #1d2022, borders #303437, white text #f5f7f8, secondary neutral gray #a1a8ae, accent fresh green #35df82. Avoid tinting all surfaces green, pastel mint, yellow olive, gradients or fake album art. Full app fits in viewport. Elegant disciplined spacing, premium modern music utility. Slim 194px left sidebar, top simple leaf mark in green next to white Grove logotype, vertically stacked left-aligned navigation Listen (selected, flat dark selected rectangle with narrow green left marker), Library, Queue, Shared jams, Connection. Main content starts at x238 y50: heading 'Your listening space', subtitle 'Play a Spotify link, or pick up where you left off.' compact outlined Connection button top right. Then a long 48px rounded link field with placeholder 'Paste a Spotify track, album or playlist link' and 'Play link' button, disabled neutral gray not dark green. Spacious now playing area below, heading 'Now playing', small gray Spotify Connect context. 180x180 black/charcoal rounded square with a crisp emerald outlined music note, subtle concentric rings like a vinyl record (code-native vector shapes). Beside it white 'Nothing playing yet', gray 'Connect Spotify to start listening'. Subtle hairline separators, then 'Up next', gray 'Your upcoming tracks will appear here.' Bottom 120px persistent player: small green leaf tile, Grove, gray Waiting for Spotify; center previous/play/next and progress slider, disabled play neutral monochrome circle; volume and Mini button right. Refined geometry: buttons 8px radius, inputs 8px radius, quiet borders only on secondary buttons, no border on selected navigation. Segoe UI typography 32px title, 24px section, 14px controls, left-aligned navigation. Native-looking clean control styling, excellent white/gray contrast, accent only for identity/active selection/actions. All labels readable, all components practical as native Slint vectors and controls. No extra nav, fake playlists, cover imagery, songs, status badges, metrics or new features.
